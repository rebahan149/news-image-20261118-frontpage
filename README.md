# The World Gazette — Wednesday 18 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118): sum up 18 November 2026's world news in one image.

A single front page that sums up one full day of world news — Wednesday 18 November 2026: **38 stories, six continents, one page**, with **13 illustrated panels**.

## What the page shows

- **13 illustrated panels**, each with an original flat-vector drawing: Washington and Tehran trade blows over Bahrain; Ukraine drops a span of the Crimea bridge; the first Quad in two years as Trump lands in Manila; Nvidia's revenue more than doubles to $115.8bn; Cyclone Akvan closes in on India's east coast; the Knesset picks Kahana, a rebuff to Netanyahu; the EU readies a €45bn loan for Ukraine; Britain sanctions settlement builders; China trims tariffs for Canada as Xi meets Carney; Dhoni calls time on his IPL career; Guinea jails 224 in a gold-mining purge; RAYE leads Grammy nominations with 11 nods; a cyberattack hits 178 of the world's biggest companies.
- **A "Where it happened" world map** with pins 1–13 matched to the panel stories.
- **25 more stories** (briefs 14–38): Australia's pay gap below 20% for the first time, Pope Leo forgiving the rosary thrower, Estonia shutting its Russia border on 1 Dec, Baidu's AI-bubble warning, Amazon's $7bn India renewables plan, Modi's free e-visas at ASEAN, the US losing measles status, Northern Ireland's Nations League rise, the Waymo freeway ban, Earthshot in Mumbai, Canada's Alert Ready test, SpiceJet's Mumbai delays — and more.

## How it was made

The page is a single self-contained `index.html` (offline fonts, inline SVG illustrations drawn for this entry) rendered to a 1024×1024 PNG. It was designed and built in-session with Muse, modelling the leaders' recipe — many illustrated panels, a map, dense briefs — with an original masthead, layout and artwork. Every headline and brief was checked against the day's reported record before the page was drawn. `main.py` emits the judged image and its dossier.
